# Creator studio imagery

Both PNGs were generated with the built-in image generator for this redesign, at 1024 × 1536 pixels, and inspected before use.

- `creator-sunset.png`: modest adult creator viewed from behind, raised arm on a rooftop overlooking a city at golden hour; amber sunlight and dark olive shadows. Used in the landing hero and illustrative script preview.
- `morning-routine.png`: modest adult viewed from behind, stretching on a cream bed beside a sunlit bedroom window; warm beige room and olive shadows. Used in the visual-scenes banner and empty state.

Art direction was grounded in the user's two reference JPEGs. Assets contain no embedded product UI, logos, promotional metrics or testimonial identities. Illustrative sample content is labelled in the app.
